Telegram Bot Integration

This service includes a built-in Telegram Bot engine that can sell products and receive Binance Pay payments right inside any Telegram chat or group.

🚀 How to connect your Telegram Bot:

  1. Open @BotFather on Telegram.
  2. Create a new bot using /newbot and copy your bot token.
  3. Add token to your .env: TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN=123456:ABC-DEF...
  4. Restart the service. Your bot will automatically start handling /start, /buy, and sending Binance Pay QR invoices!

Web API Endpoints:

POST /api/v1/payments/create
GET  /api/v1/payments/:orderId
POST /api/v1/payments/webhook
POST /api/v1/payments/refund
GET  /api/v1/payments/stream/:orderId (SSE)
